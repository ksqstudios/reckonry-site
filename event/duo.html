Reckonry 1.1

Dark mode and export, plus native layouts for iPad and iPhone Duo.

Dark mode

Reckonry follows the appearance set for your device, with a dark palette drawn for it rather than an inverted light one. The five stages read the same in both.

Export

Settings can export every reckoning as a spreadsheet and as JSON, handed to the share sheet so you choose where it goes. A reckoning still running is left out.

iPad and iPhone Duo

On a wide screen, History shows your list beside the reckoning you pick, and Home sits in a centered column.
